Privacy Policy

Stego · Last updated 19 August 2026

Stego does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind.

What Stego stores

The decks and cards you create are saved on your own device. Stego has no servers, and the developer has no ability to see your decks, your study activity, or your test results.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Stego can save your decks to your own private iCloud container so they stay in step across your devices. That data belongs to you and lives in your personal Apple account. The developer cannot access it. Apple's handling of it is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you prefer not to use iCloud, Stego keeps everything on the device instead.

Files you export

Exporting a deck writes a JSON file to a location you choose. That file is yours; Stego neither uploads it nor keeps a copy.

Data sharing

None. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone, because no data is gathered in the first place.

Analytics and tracking

Stego contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking technologies. It makes no network requests of its own and works fully offline.

Children

Stego is safe for users of any age. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, nothing is collected from children either.

Changes to this policy
