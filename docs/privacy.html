Privacy Policy

Stego · Last updated 10 September 2026

Stego works fully offline and asks for nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. A sync account is optional; without one, nothing ever leaves your device.

What Stego stores

The decks and cards you create are saved on your own device. If you never create a sync account, that is the only place they exist, and the developer has no ability to see your decks, your study activity, or your test results.

Sync accounts

You may optionally create an account so your decks are available on your other devices. Every feature of Stego works without one.

If you create an account, the following is stored on the sync server:

  • The username you choose. No email address is requested or required.
  • Your password, which is stored only as a salted hash. Neither the developer nor anyone else can read it. This also means it cannot be reset or recovered.
  • Your decks, cards and study progress.
  • Your appearance choices, but only while Sync theme is turned on for that device.

This data is used for one purpose only: showing you your own decks on your own devices. It is never used for advertising, never profiled, and never shared. It is stored on infrastructure operated by Supabase, which acts as the hosting provider.

You can delete your account at any time from Settings in the app. Deleting it permanently erases the account and everything above from the server. The decks on your device are kept, so nothing is lost locally.

iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud, Stego can save your decks to your own private iCloud container so they stay in step across your devices. That data belongs to you and lives in your personal Apple account. The developer cannot access it. Apple's handling of it is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. If you prefer not to use iCloud, Stego keeps everything on the device instead.

Files you export

Exporting a deck writes a JSON file to a location you choose. That file is yours; Stego neither uploads it nor keeps a copy.

Data sharing

None. Nothing is ever sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone. The only third party involved at all is the hosting provider that runs the sync server, and only if you choose to create an account.

Analytics and tracking

Stego contains no analytics frameworks, no advertising identifiers, and no tracking technologies. It never tracks you across apps or websites. Without a sync account it makes no network requests at all; with one, it contacts the sync server only to send and receive your own decks.

Children

Stego is safe for users of any age. It requests no personal information from anyone. A sync account needs only a username, which you choose and which need not identify you.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date.
