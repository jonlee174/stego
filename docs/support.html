Stego Support

Flashcards and practice tests for iPhone and iPad.

Need help? Email jonlee174@gmail.com. Please include your device model and iOS version so problems are easier to reproduce. Replies usually come within a few days.

Getting started

  1. Create — add a title and fill in cards. The front is the prompt, the back is the answer.
  2. Study — tap a card to flip it, swipe left or right to move through the deck.
  3. Test — pick a deck, choose your question mix, and take a graded practice test.

A new install includes a sample deck called Dinosaur Basics so there is something to try straight away. Edit or delete it whenever you like.

Studying

  • Tap the card, or press space, to flip it.
  • Got it and Review again sort cards as you go. At the end of a round you can drill just the ones you missed.
  • Show first switches between Front, Back, and Mixed.
  • The shuffle and restart buttons are in the top bar.

Building a test

You control how much of the deck is used and how many questions of each type. Four presets — Quick 10, Even mix, Write-in only, and Whole deck — are there as starting points.

TypeHow it worksNeeds
Write-inYou type the answer1 card
True / FalseJudge whether a pairing is genuine2 cards
MatchingMatch prompts to an answer bank2–8 cards per block

Cards are drawn at random each time, so no two tests are identical. A card with a blank front or back is skipped, because it cannot be turned into a question.

How answers are graded

  • Capitalisation, punctuation, accents, and extra spaces are ignored. A leading "a", "an", or "the" is ignored too.
  • Small typos are forgiven on longer answers. Answers of four characters or fewer must be exact.
  • Alternative answers — separate them with a semicolon or a vertical bar and any one of them counts as correct. For example T. rex; Tyrannosaurus.
  • Matching blocks give partial credit: one point per correct pair.
  • After submitting you get a score, a breakdown by question type, and a review of every question with the correct answers.

iCloud sync

If you are signed in to iCloud, decks sync automatically across your devices. Nothing needs switching on. If decks are not appearing on another device:

  • Check both devices use the same Apple Account and are signed in to iCloud.
  • Check Settings › [your name] › iCloud › iCloud Drive is on.
  • Sync needs a network connection. Open the app on both devices once while online.
  • If iCloud is unavailable, Stego keeps working and saves everything on the device instead — no decks are lost.

Importing and exporting

Decks live in a plain JSON file, so they are easy to back up or move. Use Export on the deck list to write a file, and Import to bring one back — either merging it with your decks or replacing them. On iPhone and iPad the file appears in the Files app under Stego.

Your data

Stego has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics, and collects nothing about you. Decks stay on your device and in your own private iCloud container, which the developer cannot access. Deleting the app removes its local data. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Requirements

  • iOS or iPadOS 14.0 or later.
  • An iCloud account is optional, and only needed for syncing between devices.
  • No internet connection is required to create, study, or test.
